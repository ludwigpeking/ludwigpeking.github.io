Richard Qian Li

Creative technologist, computational designer and educator. New York and Beijing.

I make generative systems, simulations and spatial installations: procedural cities, wave-function-collapse architecture, a projection-mapped sand table, GPU shaders, multiplayer games and decision software for real estate. Before that, twenty years in architecture, ten of them as Director of Design at Vanke, one of China's largest property developers. I teach, and I write about how cities evolve.
